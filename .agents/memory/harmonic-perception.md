---
name: Harmonic relationships versus perception
description: Scientific wording constraints for harmonic selection, normalization, and complex instruments.
---

Distinguish mathematical harmonic relationships from instrument-dependent and listener-dependent perception. GCD 1 must never be presented as a guarantee that the source pitch will be heard. Odd/even generated MIDI layers do not create strictly odd/even audio spectra with complex instruments.

**Why:** The user's harmonic-selection brief explicitly requires these distinctions and rejects claims of guaranteed perceptual pitch preservation.

**How to apply:** Preserve these caveats in future controls, reports, help text, and marketing. Sine sum-of-squares amplitude normalization must not be described as exact perceived loudness or constant-energy MIDI playback.
